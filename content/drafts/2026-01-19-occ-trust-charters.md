The OCC just approved five national trust bank charters for crypto firms.

This isn't just another regulatory headline. This is the official end of Operation Chokepoint 2.0.

In December, the OCC conditionally approved charters for institutions offering digital asset custody, settlement, staking, trade execution—even stablecoin issuance.

These aren't crypto companies asking banks for permission. These ARE banks.

What changed:
- March 2025: OCC rescinded the "supervisory nonobjection" requirement
- May-December 2025: Three interpretive letters clarifying permissible activities
- December 2025: Five charter approvals in a single announcement

For two years, crypto companies couldn't get bank accounts. Now they can become banks.

After 25 years in financial services, I've seen this pattern before. When regulators shift from restriction to clarity, the companies that prepared during the drought capture the opportunity.

The infrastructure you build during uncertainty becomes your competitive advantage when certainty arrives.

Who's positioned to move fast on this opening?
