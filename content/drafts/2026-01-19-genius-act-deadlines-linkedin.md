The GENIUS Act passed in July. The compliance clock started immediately.

Key deadlines stablecoin issuers need to know:

**January 2026:** Treasury report on illicit finance detection due to Congress

**July 2026:** Final implementing regulations expected

**January 2027:** GENIUS Act enforcement begins

**July 2028:** Non-compliant stablecoins prohibited for US persons

The FDIC just approved application procedures for banks wanting to issue payment stablecoins through subsidiaries.

What's required:
- 100% liquid reserve backing
- Monthly reserve attestations
- AML controls meeting Bank Secrecy Act standards
- No rehypothecation of reserves
- No interest payments to holders

The Tether settlement documented adequate reserves only 27.6% of the time over 26 months. That's now illegal under federal law.

After 25 years watching financial services regulation evolve, the pattern is consistent: early compliance isn't a cost—it's a competitive moat.

The 18-month transition window closes faster than most companies plan for.

Where are you in your GENIUS Act readiness?
