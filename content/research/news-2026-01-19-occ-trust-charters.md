# News Brief: OCC Grants Five National Trust Bank Charters to Crypto Firms

## What Happened
On December 12, 2025, the OCC conditionally approved five national trust bank charter applications from institutions proposing to offer digital asset products and services. This includes de novo charters and state trust company conversions. Approved activities span digital asset custody, settlement, staking, trade execution, and stablecoin issuance.

## Why It Matters
The crypto industry now has direct federal banking access without needing traditional bank partnerships. This ends the "banking desert" created by Operation Chokepoint 2.0. Crypto custody is now a federally-supervised banking activity, not a regulatory gray zone.

## Key Facts (Verified)
- December 12, 2025: OCC announces conditional approval of 5 charters
- 2 de novo national trust bank charters + 3 state conversions
- Approved activities: custody, settlement, clearing, transfer, escrow, staking, trade execution, brokerage, stablecoin issuance
- Follows rescission of Interpretive Letter 1179 (March 2025) which required SNO for crypto activities
- Additional interpretive letters in 2025: 1184 (May), 1186 (November), 1188 (December)
