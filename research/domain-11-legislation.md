## GENIUS Act: Stablecoins (SIGNED INTO LAW)

### Basic Facts

**Full name:** Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act

**Timeline:**
- February 4, 2025: Introduced in Senate
- June 17, 2025: Passed Senate (68-30)
- July 17, 2025: Passed House (308-122)
- July 18, 2025: Signed by President Trump

**Significance:** First federal legislation on digital assets to become law.

### Key Provisions

**Reserve Requirements:**
- 100% backing with liquid assets
- Eligible reserves: U.S. dollars, short-term Treasuries
- Reserves cannot be rehypothecated or pledged
- Monthly public disclosure of reserve composition
- Monthly certification by registered public accounting firm

**Who Can Issue:**
1. Subsidiaries of FDIC-insured depository institutions
2. Nonbank institutions supervised by OCC
3. State-chartered entities (if state regime "substantially similar")

**Who Cannot Issue:**
- Non-financial companies (generally prohibited)
- Exception requires unanimous approval from new Stablecoin Certification Review Committee

**Compliance Requirements:**
- Subject to Bank Secrecy Act
- Must have AML/sanctions compliance programs
- Must verify against sanctions lists
- Customer identification required
- Technical capability to freeze/seize/burn tokens when legally required

**Restrictions:**
- Cannot pay interest or yield solely for holding stablecoin
- (But other platforms CAN pay yield on stablecoin holdings)
- Members of Congress and senior officials prohibited from issuing

**Foreign Issuers:**
- May participate if from comparable regulatory jurisdiction
- Must register with OCC
- Must hold U.S. reserves sufficient for U.S. customers

### Regulatory Structure

**Two-tier system:**
- Large issuers ($10B+ outstanding): Federal regulation required
- Small issuers (<$10B): May choose state regulation if "substantially similar"

**Federal regulators:**
- OCC (primary regulator for many issuers)
- Federal Reserve
- FDIC
- Stablecoin Certification Review Committee (new body)

**SCRC composition:**
- Treasury Secretary (chair)
- Fed Chair (or Vice Chair for Supervision)
- FDIC Chair

### Effective Date

Earlier of:
- 18 months after enactment
- 120 days after final implementing regulations

### Strategic Rationale

**Dollar dominance argument:**

> "By driving demand for U.S. Treasuries, stablecoins will play a crucial role in ensuring the continued global dominance of the U.S. dollar as the world's reserve currency."

**Sources:** [White House Fact Sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-genius-act-into-law/), [Latham - GENIUS Act](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/the-genius-act-of-2025-stablecoin-legislation-adopted-in-the-us), [Covington - GENIUS Act](https://www.cov.com/news-and-insights/insights/2025/07/the-genius-act-becomes-law-key-provisions-from-the-federal-stablecoin-regulatory-framework)

## CLARITY Act: Market Structure (PENDING IN SENATE)

### What It Is

**Full name:** Digital Asset Market Clarity Act of 2025

**Bill number:** H.R. 3633

**Purpose:** Establish regulatory framework for digital assets, defining SEC vs. CFTC jurisdiction.

### Timeline

- May 29, 2025: Introduced by Rep. French Hill (R-AR)
- June 23, 2025: Advanced by House Financial Services and Agriculture Committees
- July 17, 2025: Passed House (294-134)
- Currently: Before Senate

**Senate target:** Chairman Tim Scott stated goal to advance by September 30, 2025

### Key Provisions

**Jurisdictional Division:**

| Category | Regulator | Description |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Digital Commodity | CFTC | Value "intrinsically linked" to blockchain use |
| Digital Security | SEC | Investment contracts under Howey |
| Stablecoin | Both | Depends on intermediary type |

**CFTC Jurisdiction:**
- Exclusive jurisdiction over digital commodity spot markets
- Includes centralized trading platforms
- Requires registration of digital commodity exchanges (DCEs)
- Registration for digital commodity brokers and dealers

**SEC Jurisdiction:**
- Retains jurisdiction over digital asset securities
- Investment contracts
- Tokens that meet Howey test criteria

**What's Excluded from "Digital Commodity":**
- Securities
- Derivatives
- Stablecoins (governed by GENIUS Act)

### FIT21 History

**CLARITY Act builds on FIT21:**
- FIT21 passed House in May 2024 (279-136)
- Biden administration opposed
- Died in Senate
- CLARITY Act described as FIT21 with "modest changes"

### Senate Activity

**July 22, 2025:** Sen. Tim Scott and Sen. Cynthia Lummis released discussion draft

**September 9, 2025:** 12 Senate Democrats released framework

**November 10, 2025:** Sens. Boozman (R-AR) and Booker (D-NJ) released bipartisan discussion draft

**January 2026:** Major markup session scheduled for January 15

**Key unresolved issues:**
- Whether stablecoins can pay interest to holders
- How strictly to regulate DeFi platforms
- Ethics rules around digital assets (Trump conflict concerns)
- Quorum rules for regulators

**Sources:** [Congress.gov - H.R.3633](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/3633/text), [Arnold & Porter - CLARITY Act](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2025/08/clarifying-the-clarity-act)

## Strategic Bitcoin Reserve

### Executive Order (March 6, 2025)

**Title:** "Establishment of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and United States Digital Asset Stockpile"

**What it created:**
- Strategic Bitcoin Reserve (for BTC)
- Digital Asset Stockpile (for other seized crypto)

**David Sacks description:**

> "The Strategic Bitcoin Reserve would be like a digital Fort Knox for cryptocurrency."

**Rationale:**

> "Because there is a fixed supply of BTC, there is a strategic advantage to being among the first nations to create a strategic bitcoin reserve."

### Current Holdings

**U.S. Bitcoin holdings:** ~198,000 BTC (as of August 2025)
- Largest known state holder of Bitcoin in the world
- Primarily from seizures (Silk Road, Bitfinex hack, etc.)

### BITCOIN Act of 2025 (PENDING)

**Sponsor:** Sen. Cynthia Lummis (R-WY)

**Bill number:** S.954

**Original introduction:** July 31, 2024

**Re-introduced:** March 11, 2025 (with 5 co-sponsors)

**Key provisions:**

**Acquisition target:**
- Purchase 1,000,000 BTC over 5 years
- 200,000 BTC per year
- Approximately 5% of total Bitcoin supply
- Worth ~$80 billion at current prices

**Funding mechanism:**
- Federal Reserve remittances
- Gold certificate revaluations
- Other budget-neutral strategies
- No new taxpayer burden

**Storage:**
- Decentralized network of secure facilities across U.S.
- Cold storage for government holdings

**Holding requirements:**
- Minimum holding period
- After minimum period: Secretary can recommend selling up to 10% per 2-year period
- Purpose of sales: Reducing national debt only

**Self-custody protection:**
- Affirms right of individuals and businesses to own, hold, and transact Bitcoin
- Prohibits federal interference with self-custody rights

### State-Level Activity

**16 U.S. states** have introduced state-level bitcoin reserve legislation (as of March 2025)

**Sources:** [White House - Strategic Reserve EO](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishment-of-the-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-and-united-states-digital-asset-stockpile/), [Congress.gov - S.954](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/954/text)

## SEC Leadership Change: Gensler to Atkins

### The Transition

**Gary Gensler (2021-2025):**
- Aggressive enforcement approach
- Pursued 100+ actions against crypto industry
- "Most crypto tokens are securities"
- Resisted rulemaking, preferred enforcement
- Resigned effective January 20, 2025

**Paul Atkins (2025-present):**
- Confirmed April 9, 2025
- Sworn in April 21, 2025
- Former SEC Commissioner (2002-2008)
- Co-chaired Digital Chamber's Token Alliance
- CEO of Patomak Global Partners (consulting firm)

### Philosophical Difference

**Gensler approach:**

> "Enforcement would shoot first and then ask questions later."

**Atkins approach:**

> Develop cryptocurrency regulations through "formal notice and comment rulemaking process rather than relying on enforcement actions."

### Atkins' Priorities

**May 12, 2025 speech:**
- "Rational regulatory framework" for crypto
- Clear guidelines for issuance, custody, trading
- Maintain fraud/manipulation protections
- Not presuming most tokens are securities

**Token taxonomy (non-exhaustive):**
1. Digital Commodities/Network Tokens - NOT securities
2. Digital Collectibles - NOT securities
3. [Other categories to be defined]

**On innovation:**

> "To foster innovation and to maintain President Trump's goal of becoming the 'crypto capital of the planet,' the SEC must not allow legacy rules and regulations that did not contemplate on-chain securities to act as roadblocks."

**On enforcement:**

> "This is not a promise of lax enforcement at the SEC. Fraud is fraud. If you raise money by promising to build a network, and then take the proceeds and disappear, you will be hearing from us."

### Crypto Task Force

**Established:** January 21, 2025

**Led by:** Commissioner Hester Peirce

**Purpose:** Develop comprehensive regulatory framework through public and industry engagement

**Sources:** [SEC - Project Crypto Speech](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-111225-secs-approach-digital-assets-inside-project-crypto), [Georgetown - SEC Crypto Regulation](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/ctbl/blog/beyond-enforcement-the-secs-shifting-playbook-on-crypto-regulation/)

## Collision Points

### The Speed Problem

**Technology moves faster than legislation:**
- Laws take years to pass
- Technology changes monthly
- Risk of rules being outdated on arrival

**But:**
- Framework better than no framework
- Can be amended as technology evolves
- Provides baseline for compliance

### The Bipartisan Fragility

**Current bipartisanship is unusual:**
- GENIUS Act passed 68-30 in Senate
- CLARITY Act passed 294-134 in House
- Significant Democratic support

**Risks:**
- Trump-related ethics concerns could fracture coalition
- Partisan politics could return
- 2026 elections could shift dynamics

### The Implementation Challenge

**Laws are just the beginning:**
- Agencies must write implementing rules
- Industry must build compliance systems
- Courts will interpret ambiguities
- Years before full clarity

### The International Dimension

**U.S. legislation is domestic:**
- Crypto is global
- EU has MiCA (different framework)
- Asia has various approaches
- Regulatory arbitrage remains possible

- SEC leadership changed philosophy

### What's Pending
- Market structure legislation (CLARITY Act) in Senate
