## SESSION 4 - 2026-01-14
### Starting State
- User requested complete strategic rethink
- Previous designs were "generic crap" and "postcard website"
- Stock photos not working

### Work Done
- Major strategic pivot: Plan mode to rethink entire positioning
- Created comprehensive strategy document in plan file
- KEY INSIGHT: Beth doesn't have a website problem - she has positioning/distribution problem
- NEW STRATEGY: "Fractional GC" as Trojan Horse to board seats
  - Fractional GC is more accessible than board seats directly
  - Builds trusted relationships over 6-12 months
  - Natural conversion path to board seats
  - Growing market (startups delaying full-time GC hires)
- Complete page rebuild with new positioning:
  - Hero: "Fractional General Counsel" + "Legal leadership for growth-stage tech"
  - Clear value prop: "$500K/year hire" alternative
  - Two services: Fractional GC (primary) and Board & Advisory (secondary)
  - Real photos from bethhaddock.com
  - Logos: Grayscale, Robinhood, Franklin Templeton, Guggenheim, World Gold Council
  - Media: CoinDesk, Nasdaq, Morningstar
  - Career history prominently featured
- Cleaner, simpler design: white background, substance over style
- Deployed to Vercel

### Decisions Made
- STOP saying: "Board Director & Strategic Advisor" (passive)
- START saying: "Fractional GC for Growth-Stage Tech" (active, clear service)
- Website is 20% of the work - 80% is LinkedIn, direct outreach, relationships
- Target: crypto, fintech, AI companies at inflection points
- Secondary audience: PE/VC firms who place board members

### Strategic Framework (from plan file)
- Fractional GC engagements ($15-30K/month) → trusted relationship → board seats
- Board seats come from relationships, not websites
- The website validates and converts, doesn't generate opportunities
- Focus metrics: conversations started, proposals sent, engagements closed (not website traffic)

### Starting State
- Previous Fractional GC positioning wasn't validated through brainstorming
- User pointed out superpowers skills weren't used

### Work Done
- Used superpowers:brainstorming skill properly this time
- 15+ questions asked one at a time to understand Beth's situation
- Key discoveries:
  - She wants IMPACT and OPTIONALITY (not just revenue)
  - Energizes her: strategic conversations, teaching founders, high-stakes moments
  - Drains her: operational grind, bureaucracy, being ignored, name-on-letterhead roles
  - "Fractional GC" was WRONG - too operational, drains her
  - What works: deep relationships, specific expertise asks, warm intros
  - "Being in the room" (Trojan horse) is actually her LEAST effective path
  - She's VERY PICKY - chooses opportunities, doesn't need to sell
  - She's a PEER to top founders, has SHAPED the industry (a16z portfolio companies, SEC negotiations)
  - Problem: prospects don't know what to do next, she can't/won't sell herself

### Positioning Discovered
- NOT "Fractional GC" (operational, draining)
- NOT "Board Director seeking opportunities" (passive)
- YES: Industry insider who selectively works with founders at the frontier
- The pitch: "I've helped build the regulatory frameworks that crypto operates under today"
